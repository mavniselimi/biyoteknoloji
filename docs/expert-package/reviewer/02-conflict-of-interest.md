## What a declared conflict does

It is recorded and published alongside the review. It does not disqualify you
by itself; an expert with a competing interest who says so is more useful than
one who does not.

What it does change: the project may not describe a review by a conflicted
reviewer as independent, and the WP-22 machinery has an invalidation reason —
`REVIEWER_CONFLICT_DECLARED` — for the case where a conflict emerges after
assignment.

## If you cannot review

Say so and stop. An unfinished review, honestly labelled, is worth more than a
finished one from someone who should not have done it.
